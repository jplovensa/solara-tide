Find your way

On a computer

In Walk mode, use WASD or the arrow keys to move. Drag the scene to look. Hold Shift to move faster. Q and E lower or raise the viewpoint; Escape returns to orbit.

On your phone

Use the left thumb control to walk. Drag anywhere else on the scene to look around. Use the right-hand − and + buttons to adjust eye height.

View & tour

Orbit lets you rotate the villa; pinch or scroll to zoom. Floor plan reveals a furnished section. Tour visits every listed space. Explore takes you straight into a room. Double-click a floor in the furnished plan to start walking there.

Doors retain their model placement. For the estate, choose rooms or floor levels to move between storeys; automatic stair climbing is not simulated. Press Escape to close a panel.
